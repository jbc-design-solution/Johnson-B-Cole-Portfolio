About me

I'm Johnson B. Cole, a multidisciplinary Architectural Technologist, Principal Draftsman, and Frontend Developer based in Liberia, West Africa. As CEO & President of JBC Design Solution, I lead design/build projects that fuse architectural precision with digital innovation.

My expertise spans technical documentation, 3D visualization, and concept-driven design solutions—grounded in functionality, elevated by aesthetic clarity, and shaped by deep local insight. I thrive in collaborative environments where structure, versatility, and detail matter.
